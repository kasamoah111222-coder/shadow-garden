Field manual

Resources & rules

Simple standards for reliable analysis.

◎

Verify independently

Two reports are not independent confirmation if both repeat the same original claim.

◇

Label uncertainty

Distinguish observed facts, second-hand claims, and your own interpretation.

⌁

Respect privacy

Use public or voluntarily shared information. Do not impersonate people, access private accounts, or pressure anyone to disclose confidential information.

▤

Keep records accurate

Record dates and context. Correct mistakes instead of quietly treating them as facts.

⌘

Shared data

Data is stored in the guild's Firebase project and visible to members according to their role. Do not store passwords, sensitive personal details, or genuinely confidential intelligence.

↻

Export & reset

Export a JSON backup or reset to the demo data. Keep backups somewhere safe.
